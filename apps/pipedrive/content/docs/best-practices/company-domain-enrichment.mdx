title: 'Resolve a company domain before enriching Pipedrive'
description: 'Turn a website into a defensible company match while preserving subsidiary ambiguity, profile evidence and employee-count definitions.'
---

A website is an input to account resolution, not a complete account identity. The same domain may represent a group, a regional operation or a product brand, while one operating company may own several domains. Resolve the business that your Pipedrive record represents before using a provider response to populate organisation fields or choose a sales segment.

Source: [Deepline company-domain enrichment guide](https://deepline.com/docs/guides/company-domain-enrichment). Source read date: **2026-10-18**. This original offline implementation plan includes **no live Pipedrive execution**. Source code and source prose are not reproduced.

## Deepline-specific guidance versus this Pipedrive adaptation

The source distinguishes company identity from field acceptance, checks company-profile links against website evidence and preserves the meaning of employee counts. Its named enrichment providers and checked execution path are Deepline-specific. This adaptation recommends how to carry those decisions into Pipedrive organisation records without assuming that Pipedrive performs legal-entity matching.

Pipedrive’s [Organizations API](https://developers.pipedrive.com/docs/api/v1/Organizations) documents organisation IDs, `name`, `website`, `owner_id` and updates. People are [persons](https://developers.pipedrive.com/docs/api/v1/Persons) linked by `org_id`; [deals](https://developers.pipedrive.com/docs/api/v1/Deals) carry their own `person_id`, `org_id`, `owner_id`, `pipeline_id` and `stage_id`. These record links do not prove legal-entity identity or authorise reassignment. The website is matching evidence, not a guaranteed unique key.

All `ops_` names in this guide are proposed display labels or external-ledger columns, not literal Pipedrive API keys. Resolve each approved field against the account’s [custom-field metadata](https://pipedrive.readme.io/docs/core-api-concepts-custom-fields), validate its type and option IDs, and map it to its account-specific 40-character hash. For the v2 person, organisation and deal endpoints, send mapped values inside `custom_fields`; never send an `ops_` label as a top-level payload key. Deepline integrations mentioned by the source are documented upstream, not installed or executed here. Pipedrive references were reviewed on **2026-10-20**.

## Prerequisites and licensing

Start with an approved website list, existing organisation IDs where available, a definition of the target entity and provider rights covering the intended use. Obtain a maintained domain parser and preserve the submitted URL. Decide whether the sales account is the operating company, parent or region before evaluating any candidates.

Use an authorised export and review-first proposals as the baseline. Before a later integration, verify the actual Pipedrive permissions, field capacity and selected API contracts; API access, automation access, Campaigns access and provider rights are separate. This design requires no native scoring engine, custom-code workflow action or Pipedrive CLI. It does not assume that an account has any paid automation or enrichment feature.

## Implementation steps

1. **Preserve and parse the input.** Retain the raw URL, extract the hostname and remove paths and tracking parameters from the comparison key. Keep meaningful subdomains until the identity policy permits collapsing them. Do not derive a registrable domain by taking the final two labels. Consumer email domains do not establish an employer.
2. **Retrieve existing candidates.** Prefer a saved organisation ID and read it directly. Pipedrive [organisation search](https://developers.pipedrive.com/docs/api/v1/Organizations) supports name, address, notes and eligible custom-field types; do not invent a top-level domain filter. Match website evidence against an authorised snapshot or a deliberately configured searchable text custom field, inspect all candidates, and never interpret an empty or inaccessible result as conclusive absence.
3. **Verify the entity and profile.** Compare the candidate website, trading name, geographic scope and provider record ID. Inspect a professional-network company profile's actual website context; do not invent a profile URL from a name. Record redirects separately because acquisitions and rebrands may connect different entities over time.
4. **Keep field definitions intact.** Suggested custom fields `ops_domain_match_state`, `ops_company_profile_url`, `ops_employee_range` and `ops_employee_measure` retain the decision and supporting meaning; they are proposed names, not Pipedrive defaults. Store an observed range as a range. Do not average a parent's headcount with a subsidiary estimate or use a midpoint as an exact employee count.
5. **Prepare a minimal proposal.** Pair the resolved organisation ID with accepted fields, rejected candidates, evidence dates and the original CRM snapshot. Preserve contact and deal associations. A valid profile link can be accepted while employee-based segmentation remains on hold. Any later approved update should be reread and reconciled by ID, not judged from a search result alone.

## Approval boundary and failure handling

A reviewer resolves multiple plausible accounts, company creation, parent/subsidiary interpretation and domain replacements. Existing owner, qualification state, merges and association changes are outside this enrichment plan. A routing decision that would move an account between teams requires separate approval even when the underlying evidence is accepted.

Use different states for malformed input, unresolved identity, provider failure and field ambiguity. A failed lookup must not erase a known website or profile. For a size range spanning two routing segments, retain the uncertainty and assign a reviewer rather than selecting the first matching branch. A future timeout following an update requires direct record inspection before a retry; rereading beforehand is a useful conflict check, not an atomic lock.

## Validation and operating measures

Test ordinary websites, country-code suffixes, meaningful subdomains, rebrands, shared parent domains, consumer-mailbox inputs and no-match cases. Include an employee range that spans a segment threshold. Confirm that identity acceptance and field acceptance are reported separately and that rejected candidates remain inspectable. Reconcile each input to a resolved or explicit exception state. The outcome to optimise is defensible account matches and usable routing evidence, not raw field fill rate. All validation described here is a future pilot specification, not demonstrated live Pipedrive behaviour.
