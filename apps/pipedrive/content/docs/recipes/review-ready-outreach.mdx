---
title: Prepare outreach for review, not automatic sending
description: Build a sourced draft and keep commercial claims, contact data and sending authority separate.
---

Use this recipe after qualification has produced a resolved company and a dated reason to engage. The deliverable is a draft with an evidence trail and a named approver. Sending is a separate action requiring explicit authority.

## Check that the draft is allowed

Read the dossier's gate state before writing a subject line. A blocked category or owner-decision hold is not a warning to work around. The sponsor example's renderer exits before drafting when its `draft_gate` starts with `blocked`, and also refuses an activation that has not been retrieved.

A reason about the recipient's activity must have a source URL. Missing evidence should shorten the draft, not invite generic flattery or invented familiarity. A cold target list does not establish a relationship.

## Use only settled campaign claims

Choose one relevant, evidenced activation and one campaign offer. The fixture includes rate-card tiers such as Sampling Partner and VIP Sponsor, but does not supply which tiers remain available. A draft may explain a tier's published range; it must not promise availability.

Keep estimates labelled. Omit attendance while it remains disputed. Do not invent artists, a line-up or a prior-edition track record. The sample festival's September 2026 dates are historical and must not be presented as an upcoming opportunity.

## Produce both review formats

The historical sponsor repository, not `apps/pipedrive`, exposes this command for its own checkout:

```bash
npm run deliver
```

Run it only after the local dossier and campaign have been selected and reviewed. The command assembles the packet, renders the draft, lints it, reattaches it and validates the packet. A dashboard build is optional rather than part of the default path. Despite its name, `deliver` is not an email-sending command in the implementation reviewed here.

[Source: delivery implementation](https://github.com/Git-Godssoldier/opulent-sponsor-context-showcase/blob/662ddba58c42f28ae71d0aca1a9cbb7794ec3d26/skills/opulent-sponsor-context-showcase/scripts/deliver.mjs#L47-L90).

HTML and plain text should make the same claim. Check the greeting, subject, preview, evidence link, tier and next action. A missing verified person should not be replaced by a guessed name or pattern-derived email address.

## Keep review and sending separate

The source records `draft_only_not_sent` and leaves sender authority unconfirmed. Preserve that distinction in the UI and in any CRM note. A copywriter finishing the draft cannot approve their own sending account, remove a compliance hold or certify a contact route.

A reviewer should approve the exact organisation, person, sending identity and final content, not an open-ended campaign. Use business channels only. Park uncertain identities and publicly name participants only after participation is confirmed and publication is authorised. Do not publish private contact routes in the review dashboard. A public view can show contact-verification state without exposing a usable address.

A Pipedrive person match or valid email is not evidence of permission to send. Keep `org_id`, person IDs, owner IDs and any deal links tied to the verified Pipedrive account. If the review process needs a CRM note or follow-up activity, propose it as a separate field-level change through the [local plan and API reference](/docs/reference/pipedrive-api); the draft itself does not authorise it.

## Completion check

A review-ready packet has a valid draft, cited claims, relationship path, accountable owner, status, next action, visible unknowns and an explicit not-sent state. If any required item is absent, report it rather than marking the campaign complete. For a Pipedrive write, use the [safe-write guide](/docs/guides/safe-writes): the deterministic plan needs tenant/digest-bound approval, and production execution needs a durable replay ledger and read-back check. The optional Deepline adapter is documented but not live-verified; a completed draft never bypasses that boundary.
