This is an offline demonstration of sponsor evidence and review boundaries using Pipedrive-shaped organisations, persons and deals. It is not a Pipedrive export or a live account connection. No action in this explorer sends a message, creates a record or grants permission.

<SeedExplorer />

## What to try

1. Search for **hydration** to narrow the organisation list.
2. Select **Needs a decision** and inspect Camp Current Power.
3. Notice that unknown activation evidence and unresolved exclusions remain visible even where research can proceed.
4. Search for a nonexistent organisation to exercise the empty state, then clear the search.

## From fixture to a real account

A live connection requires separate implementation and authorisation. First inspect the account schema, resolve identities and review a tenant-bound plan. Fixture identifiers and local associations are join keys only; they must never be submitted as Pipedrive record IDs or relationship writes.

Read [seed mapping](/docs/guides/seed-data), [safe writes](/docs/guides/safe-writes), the [API reference](/docs/reference/pipedrive-api) and [source register](/docs/reference/sources) before adapting this demonstration. The optional Deepline adapter is documented, not live-verified.
