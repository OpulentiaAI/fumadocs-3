This is a working, offline demonstration of the sponsor showcase’s evidence and review boundaries. It uses invented companies and contacts, not a HubSpot export. No action in this explorer sends a message, creates a record or grants permission.

<SeedExplorer />

## What to try

1. Search for **hydration** to narrow the account list.
2. Select **Needs a decision** and inspect Camp Current Power.
3. Notice that an unknown activation and unresolved exclusions remain visible even on a record whose research can proceed.
4. Search for a nonexistent company to exercise the empty state, then clear the search.

## From fixture to a real portal

A live connection is a separate implementation and authorisation step. First discover the portal schema, resolve record identities and review an explicit write plan. The fixture IDs are local join keys; they must never be passed as HubSpot record IDs.

Read [the seed mapping](/docs/guides/seed-data), [safe writes](/docs/guides/safe-writes) and the [source register](/docs/reference/sources) before adapting this demonstration.
