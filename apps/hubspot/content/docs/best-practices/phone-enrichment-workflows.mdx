This is an offline implementation plan: no live HubSpot execution, provider purchase or phone call has occurred. The objective is a defensible phone-field proposal for an identified contact. A number that parses correctly is not necessarily assigned to that person, currently reachable or permitted for outbound use. Treat those as separate questions throughout the workflow.

## Deepline-specific versus HubSpot adaptation

The source describes provider routing, validation and provenance, with exceptions for unsupported geography or uncertain identity. Deepline's provider access and orchestration remain Deepline-specific. HubSpot is the destination for accepted contact information in this adaptation, not a substitute phone verification service. The provider waterfall, acceptance thresholds, cost ledger and jurisdiction checks below are recommendations, not automatic HubSpot safeguards.

HubSpot documents contact property updates and phone-number search behaviour. Neither capability establishes personal ownership of a telephone number. Keep the evidence that led to acceptance and preserve the distinction between an inferred direct dial, a company switchboard and a user-confirmed mobile.

## Prerequisites and feature licensing

Obtain approval for the provider, permitted territories, data rights, spend ceiling and retention rules. Assign a person to resolve disputed numbers. Select an app with the necessary contact read/write permissions for a later pilot; this authoring task uses none. Confirm property capacity and entitlement in the actual HubSpot account before adding custom fields.

The [contacts API documentation](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/crm/objects/contacts/guide) supports retrieving contacts by record ID or email and updating approved properties. A separate external worker can implement the plan without assuming native workflow actions exist. If implementing provider calls through HubSpot custom-code workflow actions, the [workflow action catalogue](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions) lists Data Hub Professional or Enterprise as the requirement. Calling-tool seats, phone availability and regional restrictions need a separate entitlement check before any future calling project; they are not validated here.

## Proposed HubSpot data model

Use contact `phone` and `mobilephone` only after retrieving their destination definitions. A company telephone belongs on its company record, not automatically in every associated person's phone field. Retain the contact-to-company association as context, not proof that a switchboard is a personal number. Consult the [associations documentation](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/associations/associate-records/guide) when resolving those relationships.

Proposed custom contact properties, not built-in fields, are `phone_candidate`, `phone_evidence_status`, `phone_observed_at`, `phone_provider_ref` and `phone_review_reason`. Use an enumeration for evidence status and an appropriate datetime for observation time after administrator approval. The [properties guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/crm/properties/guide) defines property types and internal names. Keep the raw provider receipt and original formatting externally with a restricted reference; do not scatter potentially sensitive contact details through task descriptions.

## Practical implementation steps

1. Start with a resolved HubSpot contact ID and supporting identity such as verified work email and current employer. Do not perform a number lookup against a common name alone. Determine the permitted country from reliable evidence rather than guessing from the company's headquarters.
2. Check the existing number and provenance. Preserve recently user-confirmed data and honour known-invalid or do-not-contact decisions. Request only the missing phone category instead of paying for an entire contact profile again.
3. Send the minimum approved identifiers to the selected provider. Record request purpose, provider, observation time and cost. A repeated lookup for the same identity and evidence revision should reuse an acceptable retained result within your chosen freshness policy.
4. Validate the candidate's country context, number structure, extension, category and person match. Keep the raw value separate from the normalised international representation. A valid format is one acceptance component, not a claim of ownership or reachability. End the waterfall when the complete acceptance rule passes.
5. Propose a specific destination field. A personal mobile candidate should not overwrite a confirmed office line merely because both are phone numbers. Compare current values immediately before an approved write and route disagreements to review.
6. After a future authorised update, retrieve the record by its known ID and check the exact accepted property. Store the observed value and receipt. Keep calling eligibility outside this write-back step; a completed enrichment must not automatically create or execute a call.

## Approval boundary

Administrators approve the schema and field-writing policy; data governance approves provider use and territory restrictions. Human review is required for uncertain identity, conflicting confirmed numbers and moving a switchboard into a personal field. Contacting the person requires separate authority and the applicable suppression and legal checks. Do not infer that possession of a business phone establishes consent, or clear a suppression flag as a side effect of finding another number.

## Failure handling

Represent no match, malformed number, unsupported territory, provider error and disputed identity as different terminal or repair states. Do not turn a missing result into an empty-string update that erases the existing value. A transient provider failure can have a bounded retry budget; invalid identity should return to the owner instead of cycling through paid providers.

The [CRM search documentation](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/search-the-crm) states that phone searches use calculated standardisation and only area code and local number. Avoid country codes in those search criteria, but preserve country information in your own matching process. Consequently, a phone search hit is not enough to merge contacts across countries or shared office lines. Search can also lag updates, so reconcile a write timeout by direct record retrieval, not a second contact creation.

## Validation and operating measures

Prepare offline fixtures for a user-confirmed number, a shared switchboard, an international mobile with an extension error, an unsupported country, a stale employer and two contacts sharing a local number. Include repeated events and a timeout after a proposed CRM update. Verify that the workflow preserves confirmed data, records uncertain identity, selects the correct object and field, and never initiates a call.

In any separately authorised pilot, inspect read-back properties and associations as well as the external receipt. Report cost per accepted, correctly categorised field and the proportion rejected for each reason, rather than counting every returned number as useful coverage. No live behaviour, phone ownership or compliance outcome has been verified by this document.
