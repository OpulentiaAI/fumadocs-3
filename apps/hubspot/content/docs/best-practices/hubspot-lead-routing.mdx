Source: [Deepline HubSpot lead routing](https://deepline.com/docs/guides/hubspot-lead-routing). Source and HubSpot documentation read: 2026-10-18.

This offline implementation plan includes no live HubSpot execution. It uses the business term lead to mean an inbound contact under evaluation; it does not assume the separate HubSpot Leads object is enabled. The completed handoff must contain the correct owner, a reason, a deadline and a correctly associated task.

## Deepline-specific behaviour versus HubSpot adaptation

The source describes Deepline agents combining enrichment, routing and HubSpot updates. Its example territories and response times are examples, not universal HubSpot defaults. This adaptation separates those decisions into an approved policy and documented CRM operations. The routing table, SLA clock and external replay ledger are recommendations. A connector or agent does not make them automatically safe.

HubSpot documents assigning owners through user properties in the [Properties API](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/crm/properties/guide). Its [Owners API](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/owners/guide) distinguishes an owner's `id` from `userId`; ownership assignment needs the former. A team label or person's name is not the property value to write.

## Prerequisites and licensing

Get an approved territory policy, eligibility list, working-hours calendar and escalation owner. Verify contact and company access, owner-read access and the permissions needed for task creation. Confirm existing Salesforce or other owner synchronisation before introducing a competing writer.

Native [Rotate record to owner](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/assign-and-rotate-record-owners-using-workflows) is documented for Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional and Enterprise, with eligible paid sales or service seats. Its equal distribution rules for most record types are not a guarantee of sequential round robin; additional lead and ticket options have separate conditions. If that action is unavailable, use an approved external allocator or a manual queue. Do not assume custom code is included: [workflow actions](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions) places it in Data Hub Professional and Enterprise.

## Model the routing state

Read the contact ID, current `hubspot_owner_id`, approved qualification state and company association. Read the associated company's owner and validated region or segment only where the policy permits their use. Create proposed custom contact properties such as `ops_routing_state`, `ops_route_reason`, `ops_route_rule_version` and `ops_response_due_at`; confirm their schema and capacity first. These are not default HubSpot SLA fields.

Keep a ledger keyed by submission event and contact ID, storing input evidence, prior owner, proposed owner, rule version, approval and task ID. A single last-event property is insufficient for reliable event history. Compute deadlines from the agreed calendar and timezone, then store an unambiguous UTC instant rather than an unexplained number of hours.

## Implementation steps

1. **Resolve identity and eligibility.** Match the contact and inspect company associations. A duplicate or disputed account goes to review, not automatic merging. Distinguish missing enrichment from known poor fit and preserve the original submission time during retries.
2. **Evaluate rules in order.** A recommended table checks protected named-account ownership first, then accepted territory and segment, then an explicit exception route. Record the winning rule and facts. Enrich only missing inputs required for a decision; never make a critical response wait indefinitely for optional data.
3. **Resolve the actual owner.** Confirm the selected owner is active and eligible under the business policy. For native rotation, check the action's paid-seat requirements. If no eligible owner exists, assign a named operational repair responsibility rather than leaving a successful-looking unassigned record.
4. **Apply the authorised decision.** Re-read existing ownership and preserve it when the approved precedence rule says so. Serialise your allocator; read-before-write alone does not prevent a race with another workflow. Update only the approved ownership and routing metadata.
5. **Create and associate follow-up.** HubSpot's [Tasks API](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/crm/activities/tasks/guide) documents `hs_timestamp` as task due date, `hubspot_owner_id` as assignee and `hs_task_subject` as title. Associate the task with the resolved contact and, if required, the company using the correct relationship types. Persist its returned ID before reporting the handoff complete.
6. **Read back all components.** Verify owner, routing metadata, task assignee, due time and associations. A successful owner change with a missing task is a partial handoff, not an SLA-ready lead.

## Approval boundary

Approve the territory table, owner pools, existing-owner precedence and deadline policy before activation. Routine assignments can then follow that policy. Reassignment of a protected account, identity merges, lifecycle changes and external notifications require separate authority. SLA metadata and task creation do not authorise an automatic email or sequence enrolment.

## Failure handling

Route unknown territory and conflicting inputs to visible repair states. On task failure after ownership succeeds, repair only the task step. Reconcile a timeout after creation before creating another task. Track exhausted retries and cases with no eligible owner, and do not reset the original SLA start time to disguise delay. Native rotation can itself queue records, so monitor elapsed handoff time rather than assuming immediate assignment.

## Validation and acceptance

Plan tests for a protected owner, valid unowned contact, ambiguous company, ineligible owner, empty pool, repeated submission and failed task association. Compare decisions with a signed-off truth table and inspect all destination components. Measure eligible inbound records reaching verified handoff by the promised deadline, with exception age separately visible. These remain offline acceptance criteria, not measured routing results.
